# Reciprocity in AI Adoption: The Work Environment Must Adapt, Too

*People + technology*

## The Work Environment Must Adapt, Too

**01 — Make room to learn**

Time and space to understand, practice, and become comfortable with new technology.

**02 — Reduce competing demands**

Do not layer training and adoption onto an already overloaded workweek.

**03 — Redesign the workweek**

Make room for learning, experimentation, adjustment, and integration.

**04 — Reconsider expectations**

Do not automatically turn every efficiency gain into a demand for greater output.

**05 — Share the benefit**

Let AI-created capacity mean less pressure, more manageable work, and more room for life.

## The reciprocity principle

**If people are expected to adapt, the work environment must adapt, too.**

AI adoption should not be forced into an already demanding workweek.
