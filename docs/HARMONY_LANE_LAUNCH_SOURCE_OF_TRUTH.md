# Harmony Lane™ Launch Source of Truth
**Version 1.1 | November 15, 2026**

## 1. Purpose and positioning

Harmony Lane™ helps new and established founders build businesses that respect human life.

We help founders establish **Human Sustainability Boundaries™** based on the human values they want their businesses to respect, and create the **Business Operating Rules** that support those boundaries.

Our work helps founders create businesses that make room for the lives they built them for.

**Brand promise:** Your Business. Your Human Values. Your Boundaries.

**Core message:** Build a business that makes room for the life you built it for.

**Brand slogan:** We don't hustle — we harmonize.™

## 2. Who we serve

We serve two founder journeys.

### New founders building from day one

These founders are starting businesses because they want freedom, meaningful work, greater control over their time, and work-life balance.

We help them identify the human values they want their businesses to respect; establish Human Sustainability Boundaries™ before unsustainable work habits become embedded; and create Business Operating Rules that support those boundaries as the business grows.

### Established founders who unintentionally recreated demanding work habits

These founders started their businesses for freedom and work-life balance. Over time, they unintentionally carried familiar hustle habits into the businesses they built.

They may experience long hours, constant availability, excessive workload, competing priorities, and work expanding into personal time.

We help them recognize where business demands collide with their human values; examine the boundaries that need attention; establish non-negotiable Human Sustainability Boundaries™; and translate those boundaries into Business Operating Rules, communication practices, delegation, and SOPs.

Do not label these founders as “hustle-driven.” Their original intention was freedom and work-life balance.

## 3. The AI-era context

AI introduces another layer of work: new technology, opportunities, decisions, learning, experimentation, workflows, and operational changes.

Without intentional boundaries, AI and increased business capacity could accelerate existing work demands rather than create more room for life.

**Campaign message: Don't Let AI Automate Your Hustle.**

Before accelerating business operations with AI, founders should consider the human values, boundaries, and operating rules that should guide that acceleration.

AI should help create capacity—not automatically create more work.

Harmony Lane is not an AI implementation program. It helps founders reconsider the human work environment in which technology and AI adoption take place.

## 4. Core transformation framework

**HUMAN VALUES → HUMAN SUSTAINABILITY BOUNDARIES™ → BUSINESS OPERATING RULES → PRACTICE, COMMUNICATE & DELEGATE**

1. **Human Values:** Identify what matters in the founder's life, including time, attention, energy, relationships, health, personal priorities, and recovery.
2. **Boundaries:** Establish what the business must respect and protect.
3. **Business Operating Rules:** Translate those boundaries into decisions about how the business operates.
4. **Implementation:** Practice, communicate, delegate, and refine the rules through everyday business operations.

We do not promise to eliminate every business demand. We help founders make intentional decisions about what their businesses should require of them and establish operating rules that respect those decisions.

## 5. Customer journey

### Step 1 — Work-Life Balance Reality Check™

The first step in the journey and the entry point to reserving a Work-Life Balance Business Week™.

The Reality Check establishes founder and business context, identifies boundary collisions, and clarifies the areas the founder wants to address.

### Step 2 — Personalized Boundary Report™

The report helps the founder understand their results, identify boundary priorities, and see where business demands conflict with the life they want to support.

### Step 3 — Work-Life Balance Business Week™

A one-week guided founder experience where participants live, work, lead, and operate their actual businesses in an environment with non-negotiable boundaries already built in.

Founders personalize their daily experience, practice boundaries, record collisions, and begin translating selected boundaries into business operating rules.

### Step 4 — Harmony Lane™ Membership

Ongoing boundary management, review, integration, and refinement.

The founder's established boundary carries forward into membership. Monthly membership does not reset or replace it.

## 6. Business Week experience

The Work-Life Balance Business Week™ provides a guided environment for practicing work-life balance boundaries.

The established rhythm includes:
- Monday–Thursday: Four-day workweek.
- Four-Hour Focused CEO Workday™: Dedicated focused business work.
- Five hours of Time Freedom™ after the business day.
- Friday–Sunday: Three-day weekend.
- Decide & Design™: Personalize the business day around work and life priorities.
- Daily boundary check-ins: Notice and record collisions.
- Boundary Builder and Communicate + Delegate: Begin translating selected boundaries into Business Operating Rules and SOPs.

Founders bring their existing businesses, clients, projects, deliverables, workflows, and responsibilities. They are not required to adopt an entirely new business operating system.

## 7. Launch messaging principles

All launch communications must:
- Speak respectfully to founders who originally wanted freedom and work-life balance.
- Never characterize them as hustle-driven.
- Explain how demanding work habits can be unintentionally recreated.
- Explain why AI may add another layer of work and accelerate existing demands.
- Center human values as a consideration in business design and technology adoption.
- Explain the relationship between boundaries and Business Operating Rules.
- Show that founders establish boundaries appropriate to their actual lives and businesses.
- Avoid presenting Harmony Lane as an AI implementation or automation service.
- Preserve the distinction between boundaries built into the experience and boundaries personalized by the founder.
