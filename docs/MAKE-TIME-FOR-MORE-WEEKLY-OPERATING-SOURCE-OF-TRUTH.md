# SOURCE OF TRUTH — Make Time For More™ Weekly Operating Model

**Brand philosophy:** We Don't Hustle — We Harmonize Work & Life.™  
**Operating principle:** We contain work, so life has space to expand.

## The seven-day structure
- Sunday: Work-Life Balance Reality Check™; see current business/life reality and identify boundaries needing attention. Sunday is not a scheduled business workday.
- Monday–Thursday: Four scheduled business workdays. Decide & Design is repeated for each day. The founder personalizes that day's time-and-space boundaries, and those choices populate that day's schedule. The founder begins living, working, leading, and operating within the boundaries on Day 1, Monday.
- Friday–Sunday: Three-day Time Freedom weekend, with no scheduled business work. The founder practices being away from business and present in life. Sunday is also the transition into the next weekly cycle.

## The daily operating sequence, Monday–Thursday
1. Decide & Design the particular day's priorities, commitments, and boundaries. Do not assume all four days have identical schedules.
2. Personalize each boundary in its dedicated dropdown space.
3. Execute the chosen activity when the boundary's time arrives.
4. Complete a five-minute check-in at the end of each boundary to record and observe collisions.
5. Choose work for the 1:00–5:00 PM ET 4-Hour Focused CEO Workday™.

The founder can join the live Zoom co-working session for community, support, and accountability.

## Nine daily experience components
1. Flex-Time™ — prepare for the day and make room for personal priorities.
2. Decide & Design™ — personalize the day's business priorities and boundaries; repeated Monday–Thursday.
3. Morning GIV•EN™ — Gratitude, Intention, Vision & Visualization, Emotional Embodiment, and Nurture.
4. Movement Window™ — protect time for movement.
5. Extended Healthy Hybrid Lunch™ — nourishment, rest, connection, and personal priorities.
6. 4-Hour Focused CEO Workday™ — focused business work, 1:00–5:00 PM ET.
7. Time Freedom™ — business ends at 5:00 PM; personal time begins.
8. Power Down™ — live Zoom co-working, 10:00–11:00 PM ET.
9. Unplug™ — at 11:00 PM ET the community closes and the overnight digital detox begins.

## Five-minute boundary check-ins
At the end of each boundary, the founder records whether a collision occurred, what interfered, and what was observed. These check-ins are for learning, not judgment. Observations across Monday–Thursday reveal patterns and help the founder decide which boundary to integrate into the business.

## Choosing CEO work assignments
During the 1:00–5:00 PM ET focused work block, the founder chooses either:
- Existing work from her or his own business priorities; or
- An assignment from business functions such as Client Attraction, Sales & Conversion, Service/Product Delivery, Business Operations, or Business Operations Design & Redesign.

Example: choosing to create a Weekly Boundary Focus™ as a Business Operations Design & Redesign assignment. The CEO workday supports both business execution and business redesign.

## From boundary to Business Operating Rule / SOP
After practicing the four-day rhythm and recording collisions, the founder chooses a Non-Negotiable Work-Life Balance Boundary™ to integrate into the actual business. The guided Work-Life Balance Boundary Builder™ helps identify and define the boundary using the founder's observations. Communicate + Delegate™ helps determine what needs to happen, who needs to know, when the rule applies, and how to communicate or delegate it. The founder begins creating a Business Operating Rule / SOP to protect the selected boundary.

## Evening sequence — exact times
- **5:00–10:00 PM ET — Time Freedom™:** Business is already finished. These five hours are for life outside the business, not business catch-up.
- **10:00–11:00 PM ET — Power Down™:** Live Zoom co-working after the completed business day and five hours of Time Freedom. Founders acknowledge the day, reflect, and co-work on their bedtime routine. Goal: pajamas on and in bed, ready to unplug at 11:00 PM. This is not a work session and not for finishing unfinished business.
- **11:00 PM ET onward — Unplug™:** Community closes. Founders disconnect from devices and social media and protect overnight sleep; no all-night scrolling.

## Monthly membership: repetition, habits, and integration
Founders may upgrade after the one-week experience. Monthly membership repeats the same weekly rhythm so founders practice boundaries across different days and circumstances, observe collisions, refine the design, and integrate boundaries into the actual business.

The habit-building loop is: decide → design → live the boundary → observe and record collisions → adjust → integrate a selected boundary into business operations → repeat.

Two paths are available:
- **Selected boundaries:** Integrate the individual boundaries that matter most through appropriate Business Operating Rules / SOPs.
- **Full model:** Adopt the complete Work-Life Balance Business Model™, including the four-day workweek, four-hour CEO workday, daily boundaries, Time Freedom, Power Down, Unplug, and supporting operating rules.

The founder does not have to adopt the full model to benefit from membership. Any boundary selected during the one-week experience carries forward upon monthly upgrade; activation must not reset, replace, or discard it. It remains active until the founder intentionally changes it. Monthly membership adds ongoing practice, management, and review.

## Authoritative positioning
The Work-Life Balance Business Week™ helps founders redesign how they enter, experience, and operate their workweek. Starting Monday, they decide and design each business day, personalize boundaries, and immediately begin living, working, leading, and operating within them. Monday–Thursday they complete selected business assignments and record five-minute collision check-ins. They use what they learn to choose a boundary and begin creating a Business Operating Rule / SOP. At 5:00 PM business closes and Time Freedom begins; Power Down is 10:00–11:00 PM live Zoom co-working; Unplug begins at 11:00 PM. Friday–Sunday are protected Time Freedom. Monthly membership supports repetition, habit-building, and integration of selected boundaries or the full model.
